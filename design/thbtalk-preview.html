Why sparse networks fail
Three observations from two deployments
●Node dropout is bursty, not uniform.
●Static sampling wastes 40 % of the energy budget.
●Reconstruction error grows superlinearly below 12 active nodes.
Takeaway
Sampling rate should follow measured field variance, not the clock.
